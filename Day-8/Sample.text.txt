Nexora Labs is a fictional technology company founded in 2021 by Meera Kapoor. 
The company is headquartered in Hyderabad and has a research office in Bengaluru.

Nexora Labs develops AI-powered tools for education and small businesses. 
Its main product, LearnMate, is an AI learning assistant that helps students 
create study plans, summarize notes, generate practice questions, and track 
their learning progress.

LearnMate uses retrieval-augmented generation to answer questions from 
student-provided study materials. The system stores document embeddings in 
a vector database and retrieves relevant information before generating an answer.

The company has three main departments: AI Research, Product Engineering, 
and Customer Success. The AI Research team works on language models, 
embeddings, and retrieval systems. The Product Engineering team develops 
the web and mobile applications. The Customer Success team helps schools 
and small businesses use Nexora products.

Nexora Labs has 48 employees. The company follows a hybrid work model. 
Employees can work from the Hyderabad office three days a week and work 
remotely for the remaining two days.

The company's internal training program is called Nexora Academy. 
New employees attend the academy for four weeks. During the program, 
they learn Python, Git, APIs, prompt engineering, vector databases, 
and basic cloud deployment.

Nexora Labs plans to release a new product called NotePilot in 2027. 
NotePilot will convert meeting notes into summaries, action items, 
and searchable knowledge. The product will initially target small 
software companies with teams of fewer than 100 employees.
